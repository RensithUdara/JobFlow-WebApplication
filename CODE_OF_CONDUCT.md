# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

JobFlow should be a respectful and welcoming project for learning and building.

Examples of positive behavior:

- Be kind and constructive.
- Explain tradeoffs clearly.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or insults
- Discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Publishing private information

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that do not follow this code of conduct.
